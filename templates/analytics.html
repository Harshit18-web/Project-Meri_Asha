Key Dataset Insights

AI development context
⚖️

Balanced Risk Classes

The three risk categories have substantial representation in the dataset, allowing the classifier to learn patterns across different risk levels.

🧠

Feature Engineering

Raw health parameters are transformed into additional features such as MAP, pulse pressure, fever status and blood-pressure categories.

🌳

Random Forest

The trained Random Forest classifier uses the engineered health features to estimate the maternal risk category.
